Our story

SALOBRATE comes from salo-salo, the Filipino word for gathering to eat together, and celebrate. We started by cooking for family parties and kept going because people kept asking for seconds.

Today we cater birthdays, weddings, reunions and office events, serving classic Filipino dishes made with care.

Quality ingredients, delicious Filipino delicacies, and service that treats your guests like family.

Catering packages

Questions about packages and pricing

How is the price computed?

Each package is priced per guest. Delivery, setup and add-ons are listed separately in your quote.

What is the minimum number of guests?

We cater events of 30 guests or more. For smaller gatherings, send us an inquiry.

Can I swap dishes in a package?

Yes. Choose a package, then change dishes in Customize Your Event.

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks ahead is best. Weddings and large events should book a month or more ahead.

Do you accommodate dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us about allergies or preferences in the event form and we’ll plan around them.
